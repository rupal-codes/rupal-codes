Phrases of moon

  1. 🌑Blue Moon: The moon is almost invisible.
  2. 🌒Waxing Crescent: A small part of the moon becomes visible.
  3. 🌓First Quarter: Half of the moon is illuminated
  4. 🌔Waxing Glibbous: More than half is illuminated.
  5. 🌕Full Moon: The entire visible side is illuminated.
  6. 🌘Waning Crescent: The visible part slowly decreases.
